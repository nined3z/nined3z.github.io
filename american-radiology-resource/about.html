A Maryland-based imaging equipment resource built on long-standing industry relationships.

Our company

American Radiology Resource, LLC is a re-marketer of previously owned medical imaging equipment, including MRI, CT and PET/CT, and a provider of interim mobile rentals on a short- or long-term basis. Based in Timonium, Maryland, we support hospitals, imaging centers and clinics throughout the United States.

We've built strong working relationships with leading equipment, rigging and service companies across the industry, so one call to us brings the right team to your project.
